RQ3 · Hybrids do not help on held-out networks

H(λ) = λ·rank(coreness) + (1−λ)·rank(x); λ tuned on training networks, tested only on held-out ones (2-fold by domain and leave-one-out).

core + cohesion

0 held-out wins against the better single score; λ* flips between 0 and 1. Two near-identical rankings cannot complement each other. H3 not supported.

core + brokerage

Every training fold picks λ* = 0, i.e. pure brokerage. Adding coreness only hurts: the best “hybrid” is brokerage itself.

11 / 12 · Tuning and testing on the same networks would have overstated any hybrid gain

We planned a hybrid score and evaluated it properly: λ is chosen on training networks and reported only on held-out networks, both with a domain-stratified 2-fold split and leave-one-network-out. The core-plus-cohesion hybrid never beats its better component — unsurprising once we know the two are the same ranking. For core-plus-brokerage the training folds always choose λ = 0. We report this negative result openly: it is exactly the overfitting trap the held-out design was built to catch.
