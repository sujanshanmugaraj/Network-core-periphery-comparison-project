RQ2 · Brokers, not levels, find the critical nodes

Cohesion ≈ k-core: ΔR = +0.002, CI [−0.001, 0.005]. H2 not supported.

Brokers first beats k-core and cohesion in 8/8 networks; up to 44% lower R.

Gain tracks group disagreement: ρ = 0.88, p = 0.004.

9 / 12 · Betweenness still best on 4 networks; adaptive degree beats brokerage on 6 — reported honestly

Robustness: remove nodes in score order and watch the giant component. Because coreness and cohesion are the same ranking, their attacks are the same — H2 fails, and both are worse than plain degree. But a new score from the cohesion hierarchy, cohesive brokerage, the number of extra k-components a node belongs to, beats both in every network and cuts R by a third or more on Facebook, GrQc and Yeast. The gain is largest exactly where the group-level disagreement is large. It is not a universal winner: betweenness and adaptive degree remain strong baselines, and we say so. With eight networks and Holm correction no p-value goes below about 0.09, so we rely on effect sizes: rank-biserial −1 versus k-core.
