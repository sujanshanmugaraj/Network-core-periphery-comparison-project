RQ1 · Group by group, they diverge

One connected k-core can hold many overlapping k-components.

GrQc: 1 connected 2-core, 260 2-components.

Up to 7.7× more cohesive groups (power grid); none in Email-EU, C. elegans.

Gap tracks clustering and assortativity, not domain.

7 / 12 · Orange: k-components; blue: connected k-core groups (log scale)

Here is where the notions really differ. For every k we count how many connected groups the k-core has, and how many k-components. In Email-EU and C. elegans the lines coincide: every k-core group is fully cohesive. In GrQc, Facebook, Yeast and the power grid, a single k-core splits into many overlapping cohesive groups. Fragmentation grows with network size, so we do not interpret it alone. The size-normalised group Jaccard tracks transitivity and assortativity instead, and the null model on the next slide separates structure from size.
