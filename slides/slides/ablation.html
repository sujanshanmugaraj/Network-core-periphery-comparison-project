10 / 12 · All orderings remove flagged nodes first, then the rest by degree

The obvious objection: brokerage at k = 2 is just articulation points, which everyone knows are critical. This ablation answers it. Classical articulation points cut off tree branches; on the power grid removing them first is much worse than degree. On Facebook, the entire gain comes from brokers at k of 3 or more, i.e. small sets that glue together dense friend circles. On Yeast, neither part alone is enough; only the full multi-level hierarchy gets to 0.185. So the contribution needs structural cohesion proper, not just biconnectivity.
