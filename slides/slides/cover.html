Do two notions of “core” agree?

Core–periphery structure versus structural cohesion, validated by network robustness

Opening line: every network analysis eventually asks which nodes form the core. Two traditions answer differently. Core–periphery models and k-cores say the core is where ties are dense. Structural cohesion, from Moody and White, says the core is what is hardest to pull apart. Our project asks whether these two answers agree, and which one points at the nodes that really hold a network together.
