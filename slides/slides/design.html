4 / 12 · All graphs simple, undirected, largest connected component

We picked networks with very different structure: dense well-mixed social networks, highly clustered collaboration and protein networks, and a sparse spatial power grid. Everything is converted to simple undirected graphs and we keep the largest component. Methods compared: degree, betweenness, adaptive degree, k-core, Borgatti–Everett, Rombach, cohesion level and our new cohesive brokerage score. The yardstick is the Schneider robustness index R. For statistics, eight networks is a small sample, so we report effect sizes and do not oversell p-values.
